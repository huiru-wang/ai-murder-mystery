# Current product scope

AI Murder Mystery provides a playable online deduction game based on the built-in six-player script, *The Seventh Pier*. A human creates a room and chooses a public role card; the remaining seats are driven by independent AI players.

Players can read their private scripts, discuss publicly, ask a specified player a structured question, search and selectively reveal clues, finish a discussion turn and submit sealed votes. The game reveals its conclusion after the defined script rounds complete.
